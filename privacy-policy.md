# HabitLoop Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-11_

HabitLoop keeps your data on your iPhone. It does not collect, send, or share any information about you.

## What the app stores
- Your habits, check-ins, and settings (such as your default reminder time). These are saved only on your device.

## What the app does not do
- No accounts or sign-in.
- No analytics, advertising, or tracking.
- No network requests. The app works fully offline.
- No data is shared with third parties.

## Notifications
If you set a reminder, HabitLoop asks permission to send notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your device and never leave it. You can turn them off any time in iOS Settings.

## Export and deletion
In Settings you can export your data as a file you control, and delete all data from the app. Deleting the app also removes its data from your device.

## Backups
If you use iCloud or computer backups for your iPhone, iOS may include the app's data in those backups. That is handled by Apple under your own settings, not by HabitLoop.

## Children
HabitLoop collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
